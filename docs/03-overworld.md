# On the overworld

## Map travel

Open the map (`M`) and click a place, or select it and press `Enter`: your hero walks there with the game's own auto-path. Turns and encounters still happen. The map's filter buttons are remembered between sessions.

## The overworld window

A small window at the top right of the overworld (drag it anywhere; the spot is remembered):

- **Academy items:** how many Proofs and other Academy items you carry. **Go hand them in** walks you home and opens the Headmaster. With nothing to hand in, the button says **Back to Academy** and just walks you home.
- **Random nearby dungeon:** Mundane, Adventurous or Dangerous (the game's own difficulty names). It walks you to one of the 5 closest unfinished dungeons of that difficulty.

## A pig farm in every world

The town closest to the Academy always builds Pig Farmin' (the pig vendor) instead of an inn. It applies to towns you haven't entered yet.
